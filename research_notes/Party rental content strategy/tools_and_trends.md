## Key Question 1: Best scheduling/analytics tools for a solo operator posting daily to Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube Shorts — and what free tiers actually include (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
For a solo operator on IG + FB + YouTube Shorts, the practical free stack is Meta Business Suite (unlimited, free, IG+FB only) plus either Buffer's free plan (3 channels — enough to add YouTube) or Metricool's free plan (1 brand, stronger analytics). Later no longer has a free plan, only a 14-day trial.

### Cited Findings
- Buffer's free-forever plan includes 3 channels and 10 scheduled posts per channel (30 scheduled posts total at any time); it is a permanent plan, not a trial. Paid plans start at $6/channel/month — [HashtagTools comparison](https://hashtagtools.io/blog/buffer-vs-later-vs-metricool-best-scheduler-2026); [HowSociable Buffer vs Metricool](https://howsociable.com/compare/buffer-vs-metricool)
- Later offers no free plan, only a 14-day trial — [HashtagTools comparison](https://hashtagtools.io/blog/buffer-vs-later-vs-metricool-best-scheduler-2026)
- Metricool's free plan: $0/mo, 1 brand, 20 posts per month, 30 days of analytics history; LinkedIn and X are blocked on the free tier; paid starts around $25/month. Metricool's differentiator is analytics plus ads (Google/Facebook/TikTok Ads) in one dashboard — [PostFast pricing comparison](https://postfa.st/blog/metricool-vs-buffer-vs-postfast-pricing-and-features); [HeyMark Metricool vs Buffer](https://heymark.ai/en/compare/metricool-vs-buffer/)
- Positioning consensus: Buffer is best for solo creators/small teams wanting simple fast scheduling; Later is best for Instagram-/visual-first brands; Metricool is best for data-driven users who want analytics and ads in one place — [HashtagTools comparison](https://hashtagtools.io/blog/buffer-vs-later-vs-metricool-best-scheduler-2026)
- Meta Business Suite is completely free (web at business.facebook.com + mobile app): schedule posts, Reels, and Stories across Facebook and Instagram, visual content calendar, cross-posting with per-platform tweaks, unified inbox (Messenger + IG DMs + comments), and insights/analytics — you pay only for ads — [Crowbert 2026 guide](https://www.crowbert.com/how-to-schedule-facebook-posts); [Shorecraft overview](https://shorecraft.biz/meta-business-suite-simplified-facebook-instagram/); [WinnerAdSpy 2026 guide](https://winneradspy.com/meta-business-suite)

### Inferences
- Daily posting = ~30 posts/platform/month, which exceeds Metricool free (20 posts/mo total) and strains Buffer free (30 queued at once, workable if you top up the queue every few days). Practical zero-cost workflow: Meta Business Suite for IG+FB (unlimited, native), YouTube Studio's own native scheduler for Shorts, and Metricool free as the analytics layer across accounts.
- If the owner wants one third-party dashboard for all three platforms, Buffer at ~$18/mo (3 channels x $6) is the cheapest paid consolidation.

## Key Question 2: Tools to find trending audio and formats legally/safely for business accounts

### Takeaway
Business/professional accounts face real licensing limits: on TikTok, business accounts only get the Commercial Music Library, and on Instagram many chart hits are unavailable to business profiles — so the safe workflow is Instagram's in-app Trending Audio list (US professional accounts, via Professional Dashboard), TikTok Creative Center's trending-sounds charts filtered by region/industry, and original or commercially-licensed audio.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok Creative Center (ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter) has a free trending-sounds section showing which audio is gaining traction over 7-day and 30-day windows, with filters by region and industry — [TokConnect guide](https://tokconnect.com/guides/how-to-find-trending-sounds-on-tiktok/)
- TikTok business accounts see only the Commercial Music Library when adding sound; chart hits and the sounds behind most trends are not included. Trends built on original audio, covers, or generic sounds may still be usable — [Status/brands guide](https://brands.joinstatus.com/tiktok-commerical-music-library)
- Instagram's Trending Audio list is accessible from the Professional Dashboard for US professional accounts; trend-spotting can also be done via Reels feed and the up-arrow "trending" indicator on audio — [Veed guide](https://www.veed.io/learn/trending-audio-on-instagram)
- Why restrictions exist: platforms license music from labels/publishers, and those licenses are typically broader for personal entertainment use than for commercial content from companies — so many trend audios are simply unavailable to business profiles — [Createsome explainer](https://www.createsome.de/en/insights/social-media-management/instagram-music-for-businesses)
- Free weekly trend roundups exist that list currently-trending IG audio with usage ideas (e.g., Scott Social's "50+ Trending Reels Audio This Week," updated for October 2026, and SocialBee's weekly trending-songs post) — [Scott Social](https://www.scottsocialmarketing.com/blog/trending-reels-audio-this-week-on-instagram); [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/trending-instagram-songs-this-week); [Later's weekly-updated Reels trends page](https://later.com/blog/instagram-reels-trends/)

### Inferences
- For a brand with an animated cartoon character series, original audio (voiceover by the character) sidesteps licensing entirely and is actually an advantage: trends built on original/spoken audio formats can be recreated legally, and original audio that takes off becomes brand IP.
- A free weekly 15-minute routine beats paid trend tools (e.g., Trendpop, which targets enterprise) at this scale: check IG Professional Dashboard trending audio + one weekly trend roundup (Later or Scott Social) + TikTok Creative Center.

### Gaps
- Trendpop's current pricing/features were not researched in depth (no reliable 2026 source surfaced in searches); it is an enterprise-leaning TikTok analytics tool and likely overkill for a solo operator, but this is inference, not verified 2026 fact.
