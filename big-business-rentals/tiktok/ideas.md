Labels: VERIFIED (our own numbers from TikTok Studio or Buffer), OBSERVED (seen in a source or
on the platform, not measured by us), ESTIMATED (a number someone else claims), HYPOTHESIS

## Oct 20, 2026 · baseline findings from our own data

- VERIFIED (Buffer, 145 sent TikTok posts, matches the owner's Studio baseline): median 15
  views per post, 43% of posts at 5 views or fewer, 0 comments on every post.
- VERIFIED (Buffer, by old lane): real-footage posts median 122 views, 4.6s average watch;
  old PRICE IT posts (AI image plus a question) median 72 views, 5.0s average watch;
  Chronicles episodes median 32 views, 2.9s; gag reels median 22, 3.8s; pink awareness
  median 6, 1.2s; graphic card carousels median 2 to 3 views.
- VERIFIED (Buffer, October by day): 8-post days drew 9 to 84 total views except Oct 6 (794);
  3 to 5 post days drew 49 to 485. Agrees with the owner's Studio read: fewer posts, more views.
- HYPOTHESIS: the PRICE IT result (AI concept image plus a real question held 5.0s) is the
  best early signal that Guess the Theme can hold attention. First real test: Sat Oct 24 Pt. 1.

## Oct 20, 2026 · outside research

- ESTIMATED: completion and watch time are the strongest ranking signals, then shares,
  saves and comments, then likes. Several guides put the "push" line around 70% completion;
  TikTok publishes no number. Sources:
  [Darkroom](https://www.darkroomagency.com/observatory/tiktok-algorithm-guide-2026-everything-we-know-about-how-videos-are-ranked),
  [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-the-tiktok-algorithm-works),
  [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/blog/tiktok-algorithm-2026-what-works-now).
- OBSERVED: guides recommend putting the topic keyword in three places, the on-screen hook
  text, the spoken audio and the caption, and keeping language, location and content pointed
  at one region. Our search traffic was off-topic, so the captions should name the thing
  ("party," "Las Vegas," "hosting") in plain words.
  [Transformation Junction](https://www.transformationjunction.com/blog/how-to-rank-on-tiktok-2026-algorithm/),
  [Akselera](https://akselera.tech/en/insights/guides/tiktok-algorithm-fyp-guide).
- OBSERVED: a retention graph with a cliff in the first 2 seconds points at the hook; this
  matches our 0:01 drop exactly.
  [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/blog/tiktok-algorithm-2026-what-works-now).
- OBSERVED: TikTok's 2026 Discover List featured creators doing TV-style episodic work,
  including a mini-sitcom series, which supports keeping the Chronicles as a recurring series.
  [TV Insider](https://www.tvinsider.com/1263896/tiktok-2026-discover-list-tv-drew-talbert-jeffrey-morrison-taryn-delanie/).
- OBSERVED: single relatable relatives (the aunt, the uncle at the gathering) keep going
  viral as one-joke clips, and family-sketch accounts grow on "relatable family life."
  Supports the Hosting Chaos pillar.
  [Briefly](https://briefly.co.za/people/family-relationships/188728-hilarious-aunt-refuses-wig-leaves-mzansi-stitches-shares-video/),
  [Motion](https://motionapp.com/creator/meryemserhatada/).
- OBSERVED: AI-character creators are told to keep the character's face and design locked
  across episodes ("character drift" breaks recognition). Our rig character never drifts;
  keep it that way.
  [AnimateAI](https://animateai.pro/blog/how-can-ai-animation-transform-your-tiktok-content-strategy).
- OBSERVED (Oct 19 trend roundups, logged in posting_captions.md): black-cat reaction clip,
  Halloween porch reveals, costume slow-reveals. All three fit our cast.
- Not found: no source measured a "guess the theme" two-part decor format. Our own Pt. 1 and
  Pt. 2 numbers will be the first data on it.
