PARTY MATH · TIP No.8

3 LAYOUT MISTAKES
THAT KILL THE VIBE

1

TRAPPING THE FOOD ✗

Buffet shoved in a corner = one long line through the seats. Pull the 6ft table off the wall so the line moves down both sides.

2

NO WALKING LANES ✗

Guests shouldn't squeeze past chairs all night. Leave 5 ft between round tables — chairs pull out, people flow.

3

DANCE FLOOR LAST ✗

Place it FIRST, next to the DJ — then seat around it. Nobody dances in a hallway between tables.
