## 1) THE DIALOGUE (Text to Speech / v3 with audio tags)

Create it as a two-speaker dialogue. Voice settings for both:
Stability ~30% (expressive), Similarity ~80%, Style ~40%.

**VOICE 1 — "HER"** (pick a smooth, confident female voice — sassy, unimpressed):
```
[skeptical] So… [pause] what exactly do you bring… to the table?
```

**VOICE 2 — "HIM"** (pick a deep, smooth, confident male voice):
```
[amused] Funny you should ask that. [pause] [smug] Baby… I bring the TABLES.
[confident] Round ones. Six-footers. [chuckles] Chairs too.
```

**VOICE 1 — "HER" (reaction):**
```
[bursts out laughing] Oh my god. [laughing] You cannot be serious right now.
```

**Optional deep movie-trailer VO for the end card (Voice 2 or a trailer voice):**
```
[deep, dramatic] Big Business Party Rentals. [pause] We bring the tables. Literally.
[warm] Seven-oh-two… seven-oh-six… eighty-two eighty-seven.
```
