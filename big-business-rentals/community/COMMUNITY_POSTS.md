## Before you post: the rules that keep these up

1. **Read each group's rules first.** Many groups allow business posts only on a set promo day or
   in a pinned promo thread. A post in the wrong place gets deleted and can get you removed.
2. **Rotate the captions.** Use a different caption and flyer in each group instead of pasting the
   same post everywhere on the same day. Facebook can treat identical copies as spam.
3. **Use community and event groups, not buy-and-sell groups.** Meta's commerce rules (which
   cover Marketplace and buy-and-sell groups) don't allow rentals or services. Neighborhood,
   moms, party planning, quinceañera, church and local event groups are the right fit.
4. **Never use the Page's pinned Instagram/Facebook follow comments in groups** (standing rule).
5. **Answer every comment fast.** A reply within the hour is what turns a comment into a booking.
   Reply templates are at the bottom.
6. **Write down which groups bring calls or messages.** Bring the list Sunday and we'll keep the
   groups that produce leads and drop the ones that don't.

## Facebook Marketplace: hold off

Meta's commerce rules say Marketplace listings must be physical items for sale, and they don't
allow services or rentals. A rental listing will likely be removed, and repeated removals can lock
you out of Marketplace. Groups and Nextdoor carry the same flyers without that risk. If you ever
sell gear outright (retired chairs or tables), that can go on Marketplace.
