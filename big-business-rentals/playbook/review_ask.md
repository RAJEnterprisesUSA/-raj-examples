# Google review ask · neutral, no reward, every customer

Rules (FTC 2024 fake-review rule + what local buyers trust):
- Ask EVERY completed customer, happy or not. Never filter out people you think will be unhappy.
- Never offer a discount, gift, entry or referral credit for a review, and never ask for "5 stars".
- Ask within a day of pickup while the party is fresh. Recent reviews matter most to local shoppers.
- Reply to every review personally, name the event type, and sign with your first name. No copy-paste replies.

## Text / DM (the day after pickup)
Hi [NAME], thanks again for having us at your [EVENT]! If you have a minute, would you tell others how it went on
Google? It really helps a small local business. [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK] · [Your name], Big Business Party Rentals

## En español
¡Hola [NOMBRE]! Gracias otra vez por tenernos en tu [EVENTO]. Si tienes un minuto, ¿nos ayudas contando en Google cómo
te fue? Ayuda mucho a un negocio local. [LINK DE RESEÑAS] · [Tu nombre], Big Business Party Rentals

## Reply examples (change them every time)
- 5 stars: "Thank you, [NAME]! That backyard [quince / birthday / baby shower] came together beautifully. We'd love to
  set up the next one. · [Your name]"
- Mixed: "Thanks for the honest feedback, [NAME]. You're right about [ISSUE], and we've [WHAT YOU CHANGED]. We hope to
  make the next one perfect. · [Your name]"
