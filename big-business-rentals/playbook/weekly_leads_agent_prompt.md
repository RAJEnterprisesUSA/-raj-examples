# Prompt for the weekly leads agent

Copy everything below the line and give it to your agent each Sunday (change the dates each week).

---

You are helping Big Business Party Rentals & Events (Las Vegas) measure which social media posts bring in real customers. Each Sunday, collect this week's lead information from the sources below and report it in the exact format at the end. Do not contact any customers, do not reply to anyone, and do not change anything in the accounts. Only read and count.

**This week:** Monday Oct 26, 2026 through Sunday Nov 1, 2026 (Las Vegas time).

**What counts as a lead:** any person who asked about renting tables, chairs, linens, tents, setup or the venue, or asked for a price or a quote, during this week. Count each person once, even if they reached out more than once or in more than one place. Do not count spam, sales pitches, job seekers or existing bookings asking routine questions (those go under "Other").

## Where to look

1. **Phone:** the business phone's call log and texts (702-706-8287). Count inbound calls and texts from new people asking about rentals.
2. **Facebook:** the Page inbox in Meta Business Suite (Messenger) and comments on Page posts asking about prices, dates or availability.
3. **Instagram:** the DM inbox (including the Requests folder) and comments asking about prices, dates or availability.
4. **Website chat:** the chat on bigbusinesspartyrentals.com (its conversation history or email notifications).
5. **Google Business Profile:** messages, calls and direction requests shown in the Performance section, plus any new reviews.
6. **Facebook groups and Nextdoor:** replies or messages that came from our group posts.
7. **Referral partners:** anyone who said a barber, stylist, nail tech, DJ, bartender, photographer or party planner sent them.

## For each lead, record

- **Day** the lead first reached out.
- **Channel:** Phone call, Text, FB message, FB comment, IG DM, IG comment, Website chat, Google, FB group, Nextdoor, or Other.
- **What they mentioned seeing,** if they said it: for example "the price list," "the holiday flyer," "the Thanksgiving video," "the cartoon," "Facebook group post," "Google," "a friend," or "unknown." Write their words if short. If they did not say, write "unknown". Do not guess.
- **Event type and date,** if given (birthday, Thanksgiving, quinceañera, office party, and so on).
- **Headcount,** if given.
- **Referred?** Yes (write the partner's job, not their name) or No.
- **First reply time:** how long until someone from the business first replied (under 1 hour, 1 to 4 hours, same day, next day, or no reply yet).
- **Result:** Booked, Quote sent, Still talking, No response from customer, or Not a fit.
- **Did the post they mention show prices?** Yes, No, or unknown.

**Privacy:** do not write down phone numbers, full names, addresses or screenshots of private messages. A first name or "Lead 1, Lead 2" is enough.

## Also report

- **Google reviews:** how many new reviews came in this week, and their star ratings.
- **Website chat:** how many chats total, and how many were real leads.
- **Anything unusual:** a post people kept mentioning, a common question, or a complaint.
