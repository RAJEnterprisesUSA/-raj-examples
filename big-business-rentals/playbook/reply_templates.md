# Reply templates · DMs, website chat, comments, group posts

Goal: reply within ONE HOUR during business hours. Leads that get an answer in the first hour are far more likely to
turn into a real conversation than leads answered later (Psychology for social growth report). Every reply asks for the
same two things: the DATE and the HEADCOUNT. Offer no more than 2 to 3 options. Sign with your first name.

Prices to use (owner's card + owner-approved math, Oct 26). Delivery is always quoted with the order.
- Folding chair $2 · 6 ft rectangle table $10 · 6 ft round table $15 · table + 6 chairs set $22 (seats 6)
- 4 sets $88 (24 guests) · 7 sets $154 (40 guests) · 8 sets $176 (48 guests) · 10 sets $220 (60 guests)
- Full-service setup and breakdown: packages from $265
- Private venue: 3 hours $625 (up to 25 guests) · 4 hours $800 (up to 40 guests) · seats 40, holds 80 standing
- Never call the $22 set a discount. Never promise a date is open until you check.
