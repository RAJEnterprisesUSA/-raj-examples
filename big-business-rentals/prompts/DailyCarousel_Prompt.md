# PROMPT: Daily Carousel Post (replaces the single static card)

Do not put the whole message on one card. Design each daily post as a swipeable carousel of
4 to 6 slides (1080x1350 preferred), where each slide carries ONE idea and gives the viewer
a reason to swipe to the next.

## Slide structure
1. HOOK slide. Huge headline only, a question or bold claim, about 8 words or fewer.
   No body text. Small "SWIPE" cue. This slide decides whether anyone stops scrolling.
2. SETUP slide. Name the problem or fact the hook promised, in one or two short lines.
   Small progress marker such as "2/5".
3. VALUE slides (1 to 3). One tip, number, or step per slide. Large number or keyword in gold,
   one supporting sentence, simple illustration or ghost numeral behind it.
   Never more than two sentences of text.
4. PAYOFF slide. The surprising result or "here is what to do" line, set bigger than the
   value slides so it feels like a reward for swiping.
5. CALL-TO-ACTION slide (last). One clear action: "Comment VENUE and we will DM you the details"
   or "Send this to whoever is planning the party." Then phone number, website, Instagram handle,
   LAS VEGAS, NV, and the logo.

## Design rules for every slide
- Same palette and fonts across the whole carousel so it reads as one piece, a different palette each day
- Heavy display type for headlines, elegant italic serif for accents, clean sans for small text
- Gold accent on every slide, thin gold frame, film grain, soft vignette
- Slim progress bar or "2/5" counter on every slide after the first
- Slide 1 and the last slide each work on their own if someone only sees one
- Brand footer (logo, 702-706-8287, bigbusinesspartyrentals.com) small on every slide, large on the CTA slide
- No em dashes. No photos of real people.

## Caption for the carousel
First line is a question or bold claim, then one line telling people to swipe, then a
"send this to..." line or ONE keyword CTA, then the contact line and hashtags.

## Why this works
Every swipe counts as an interaction, finishing the carousel signals value, and the CTA slide
gives people a specific thing to send, save, or comment.

## Example: Tip No.22 "Ice Math"
- Slide 1: "HOW MUCH ICE DO YOU ACTUALLY NEED?" with a SWIPE cue
- Slide 2: "Most hosts guess. Most hosts run out by 9pm."
- Slide 3: "2 lbs per guest" in huge gold numerals
- Slide 4: "Hot night? Make it 3." (the Vegas heat angle)
- Slide 5: "The cooler count" with how many coolers for 50 guests
- Slide 6: CTA, "Send this to the friend who always forgets the ice" plus the contact block
