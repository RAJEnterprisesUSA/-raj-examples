# PROMPT: "Cast File" Character Reveal Series (instead of one static group card)

Do not make a single group cast poster. Instead, design a series of 6 individual character
introduction cards, one per character, released as a swipeable carousel or one per day,
so each cast member gets their own moment and the audience picks favorites.

## Shared layout for every card (vertical 1080x1350, 4:5)
- Dark cinematic background in a radial gradient, with film grain texture, a soft vignette,
  and a thin gold frame with small gold diamonds in the corners
- A giant "ghost" file number (01 to 06) rendered as a transparent outlined numeral behind the content
- A rotated gold stamp badge top right reading "CAST FILE No.0X"
- Small letterspaced kicker at top left: "THE BIG BUSINESS CHRONICLES · SEASON ONE"
- The character's name huge in heavy display type (Archivo Black style), with one word in a gold gradient
- Under the name, a one-line signature tagline in elegant italic serif (Playfair style)
- A "stat sheet" of 3 rows, each with a small gold label and a bigger value line, written like
  comedy trading-card stats
- The character illustrated in flat cartoon style standing bottom right with a soft drop shadow
  and floor shadow ellipse
- Footer across the bottom: company logo, 702-706-8287, bigbusinesspartyrentals.com,
  @bigbusiness_rentals_events, LAS VEGAS, NV
- Each card uses a DIFFERENT color world, but gold appears in all of them so the series
  still reads as one brand

## The six cards
1. MR. BIG BUSINESS (royal purple world): slim Black man with a faded haircut, mint green tee,
   gold chain. Tagline "The brand. The man. The fade." Stats: ROLE: CEO of everything /
   SPECIALTY: Doing it himself / RECORD: Wins pending
2. MRS. NEVER WRONG (deep burgundy world): Black woman, high bun, gold hoop earring, pink tee.
   Tagline "She already told you." Stats: ACCURACY: 100 percent, undefeated /
   WORDS PER EPISODE: About three / WEAPON: The eyebrow
3. UNC (black and gold world): older Black man, purple backwards cap, cream tee, leaning back slightly.
   Tagline "He knows a shortcut." Stats: LISTENS: Never / CHAIR LEAN: Illegal angles / SUPERSTITION: Maximum
4. TYLER THE INTERN (dark teal world): slim fair-skinned young guy, orange backwards cap, blue tee,
   khakis, wide eager eyes, waving. Tagline "...im the new intern." Stats: WEEK ON THE JOB: One /
   INSTRUCTIONS: Taken literally / ARRIVES: Out of nowhere
5. LIL WOBBLES (midnight navy world, villain energy): a cheap red folding chair, slightly tilted,
   presented like a menacing character. Tagline "Every party has a villain." Stats: LEGS: Four, allegedly /
   HOLDS WEIGHT: Absolutely not / DEFEATED: Never. It returns
6. THE CAT (dark Halloween purple world): black cat silhouette with green eyes, sitting in front of a
   glowing full moon so it reads against the dark. Tagline "Silent. Judging. Seated."
   Stats: WORDS SPOKEN: Zero / BEST SEAT: Already taken / LUCK PROVIDED: Bad

No em dashes anywhere. No photos of real people. Keep the character art in a flat,
thick-outline cartoon style consistent across all six cards.
